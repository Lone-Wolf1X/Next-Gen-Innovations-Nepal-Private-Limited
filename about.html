About Next Gen

An emerging Nepali IT company on a mission to bring cutting-edge technology solutions to Nepal and beyond.

Our Story

Built in Nepal.
Built for the World.

Next Gen Innovations Nepal Private Limited was founded with a singular belief: that Nepali talent is capable of delivering world-class technology solutions. We started as a small team of passionate developers, united by the dream of building a company that would put Nepal on the global IT map.

Today, we specialize in FinTech, enterprise software, mobile apps, AI research, and cloud infrastructure — serving clients across Nepal and internationally. Our work is driven by a relentless pursuit of quality, innovation, and impact.
