1. Scarcity in economics refers to:
अर्थशास्त्रमा Scarcity भन्नाले के बुझिन्छ?
A. Unlimited resources
B. Resources being limited relative to unlimited wants
C. Absence of demand
D. Excess supply
Answer: B
